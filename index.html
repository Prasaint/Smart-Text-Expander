Smart Text Expander

Smart Text Expander is a browser extension that replaces short shortcuts with longer text automatically. Save time, reduce repetitive typing, and boost productivity while writing emails, messages, forms and code.

View on GitHub

Features

🚀 Instant Expansion

Replace shortcuts with complete text instantly while typing.

💯 Free Forever

No subscriptions, no premium plans and no usage limits.

🔒 Privacy First

Your snippets stay on your device. No tracking or analytics.

⚡ Productivity Boost

Perfect for emails, forms, customer support and coding.

Example

Type:

/addr

Automatically becomes:

Kathmandu, Nepal

Privacy

Smart Text Expander stores your snippets locally in your browser. No personal information is collected, transmitted, shared or sold. The extension does not use analytics, tracking technologies, advertisements or external servers.
