Privacy Policy

Smart Text Expander

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

Smart Text Expander stores your snippets locally on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, sold, shared, tracked, or analyzed.

What the extension stores

  • Snippets and categories
  • Extension settings
  • Usage statistics (without storing typed text)

What the extension reads

The extension temporarily checks text near the cursor to detect shortcuts. This data remains in memory only and is never stored or sent.

Permissions

  • Storage — Save snippets and settings locally.
  • Website Access — Enable shortcut expansion on websites.
