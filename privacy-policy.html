Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary

Smart Text Expander is free. It stores your snippets on your own device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What the extension stores

Saved in your browser with chrome.storage.local, on your device only:

  • Your snippets (name, shortcut, text, category) and categories.
  • Your settings (on/off, case sensitivity, trigger keys).
  • Usage counts: how many times each snippet was used, and a total per day. These contain no typed text.

What the extension reads

To expand shortcuts, a content script runs on web pages and reads the text just before your cursor in memory only, to check whether it matches one of your shortcuts. That text is not saved, logged or sent anywhere. The extension ignores password fields and fields that look like passwords, one-time codes, PINs or card numbers.

What the extension sends

Nothing leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests and has no servers.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your snippets and settings locally.
  • Access to all websites (content script): lets your shortcuts expand in text fields on any site you use. It is not used to read, collect or store page content.

Your control

You can export your snippets as a JSON file at any time. "Clear all data" in Settings deletes everything. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data from your browser.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be included in the extension and the date above will be updated. If a future version ever sends data off your device, this policy will say exactly what is sent and why before that version is released.
