# Privacy Policy - Smart Text Expander

_This is the same policy as `privacy.html` inside the extension. The Edge Add-ons store needs a public URL, so host this text (for example on GitHub Pages) and paste that URL into your listing._

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026  
**Contact:** prashantkhadkapass@gmail.com

## Summary
Smart Text Expander is free. It stores your snippets on your own device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share your data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What the extension stores
Saved in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`, on your device only:
- Your snippets (name, shortcut, text, category) and categories.
- Your settings (on/off, case sensitivity, trigger keys).
- Usage counts (how many times each snippet was used, and a per-day total). These contain no typed text.

## What the extension reads
To expand shortcuts, a content script runs on web pages and reads the text just before your cursor **in memory only**, to check whether it matches one of your shortcuts. That text is not saved, logged or sent anywhere. The extension ignores password fields and fields that look like passwords, one-time codes or card numbers.

## What the extension sends
Nothing leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests.

## Permissions
- **storage** - to save your snippets and settings locally.
- **Access to all websites (content script)** - so shortcuts can expand in text fields on any site you use. It is not used to read or collect page content.

## Your control
Export your snippets as JSON at any time. "Clear all data" in Settings deletes everything. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored data from your browser.

## Future changes
If you later add features that send data off the device (such as cloud sync), update this policy before releasing them. State exactly what is sent, where it is stored, and how users can delete it.

## Children
The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

## Changes
Any changes will be included in the extension and the date above updated.
